============================================ 📄 Welcome to DocuMint ========================================== 

Your trusted platform to turn documents into NFTs with security, transparency, and ease!

👉 What is DocuMint?
    DocuMint is a cutting-edge Web3 platform designed to convert your important documents—including personal, educational, legal, and official records—into Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs).

    By leveraging blockchain technology, we ensure that your data remains secure, tamper-proof, and easy to access.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🎯 Purpose of DocuMint
    The platform addresses real-world challenges like:

▶Document Security:         Eliminates risks of tampering or loss.
▶Ownership Assurance:       Ensures you retain sole ownership of your documents via unique NFT IDs.
▶Seamless Accessibility:    Access your documents anywhere, anytime, securely on the blockchain.
▶Example:
        Imagine you lose a physical birth certificate. With DocuMint, you can always access its verified NFT version from anywhere globally.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------        
💡 Key Features
            🔒 Secure Storage: Your documents are stored off-chain on IPFS (InterPlanetary File System) and linked to NFTs on Ethereum.

            ⚡ Fast Conversion: Upload a document, and within seconds, it becomes a verified NFT.
            
            👤 User-Friendly Dashboard: Easily track, manage, and view your NFT documents.

            📜 Transaction History: Stay updated with a clear record of all your activities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🛠️ Technologies Used
            Frontend: React.js (for a responsive and interactive user interface).
            Backend: Node.js, Express.js (to handle requests and API logic).
            Database: MongoDB Atlas (to store user details and transaction records).
            Blockchain: Ethereum (for NFT minting) and IPFS (for off-chain document storage).
            Middleware: Multer (to handle file uploads).

📩 FAQ
1. What file formats are supported?
We support PDF, DOCX, JPEG, and PNG files.

2. What happens if I lose my NFT?
Your NFT is securely tied to your blockchain wallet. As long as you have wallet access, your document remains safe.

3. How is my data stored?
Your files are stored on IPFS, a decentralized storage system, ensuring both privacy and reliability.

4. Is there a fee for converting documents to NFTs?
Yes, there’s a small fee for blockchain minting and storage services.

5. Can I edit or delete an NFT document?
NFTs are immutable, but you can update metadata by uploading new versions or minting a fresh NFT.
